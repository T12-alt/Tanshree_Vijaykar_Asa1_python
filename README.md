#  E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an E-Commerce sales dataset to identify business insights related to revenue performance, customer behavior, logistics efficiency, product demand, and customer satisfaction.

The objective is to leverage data analytics techniques to uncover trends, optimize operations, and support data-driven decision-making for sustainable business growth.

---

##  Business Objectives

The analysis aims to address the following key business challenges:

* Analyze revenue performance and sales trends.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Evaluate logistics and fulfillment efficiency.
* Investigate order cancellations and returns.
* Provide actionable recommendations to improve business performance.

---

##  Dataset Information

The dataset contains order-level information from an e-commerce platform, including:

* Order ID
* Date
* Order Status
* Fulfillment Method
* Product Category
* Product Style
* SKU
* Quantity Ordered
* Revenue (Amount)
* Customer Location
* Courier Status
* B2B Indicator
* Promotion Details

---

##  Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

The following data preparation steps were performed:

* Removed unnecessary columns.
* Identified and handled missing values.
* Replaced null values in the Amount column.
* Converted date columns into datetime format.
* Created additional time-based features for trend analysis.
* Verified data quality and consistency.

---

##  Analysis Performed

### 1. Financial Performance Analysis

* Total Revenue Analysis
* Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Revenue by Product Category
* Top Revenue-Generating Products

### Key Findings

* April 2022 generated the highest revenue.
* Set and Kurta categories contributed the largest share of revenue.
* Revenue showed strong concentration in a few high-performing categories.

---

### 2. Customer Insights Analysis

* Top Revenue-Generating States
* Top Revenue-Generating Cities
* B2B vs B2C Customer Analysis
* Order Cancellation Analysis

### Key Findings

* Maharashtra generated the highest revenue.
* Bengaluru and Hyderabad were the top-performing cities.
* B2C customers accounted for the majority of orders.
* Cancellation rate was approximately 14.22%.

---

### 3. Logistics & Fulfillment Optimization

* Fulfillment Method Comparison
* Courier Status Analysis
* Shipping Performance Evaluation

### Key Findings

* Amazon fulfillment handled most orders and revenue.
* A significant number of orders were marked as Unshipped or Unknown.
* Logistics monitoring can be improved through better shipment tracking.

---

### 4. Product Management & Inventory Optimization

* High-Demand Product Categories
* Best-Selling SKUs
* Product Style Performance
* Average Quantity Ordered

### Key Findings

* Set and Kurta were the most demanded categories.
* JNE3797 emerged as the highest-performing product style.
* Customers typically purchased one unit per order.

---

### 5. Customer Satisfaction & Returns Analysis

* Return Analysis
* Cancellation Analysis
* Revenue Lost Due to Cancellations
* Product Categories with High Returns

### Key Findings

* More than 2,000 orders were returned or in the return process.
* Revenue loss due to cancellations exceeded ₹11.5 million.
* Set and Kurta categories recorded the highest return and cancellation volumes.

---

##  Recommendations

* Increase inventory allocation for high-performing categories.
* Improve order fulfillment and shipment tracking processes.
* Reduce cancellations through better inventory management.
* Enhance product descriptions and size guides to minimize returns.
* Focus marketing efforts on top-performing states and cities.
* Expand B2B partnerships to leverage higher average order values.

---

##  Project Outcome

The analysis provides valuable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, logistics efficiency, and product demand. The findings can help the business improve operational efficiency, enhance customer satisfaction, and support long-term sustainable growth.
